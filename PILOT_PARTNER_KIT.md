# SkillFi Arena — Pilot Partner Kit

## Purpose

Use this pack to secure two credible, written design-partner commitments before the Circle Developer Grants application. The pilot is testnet-only and must never be presented as gambling, an investment opportunity, guaranteed income, or a production financial service.

## 30-second partner pitch

SkillFi Arena is building a transparent USDC settlement layer for peer-to-peer skill competitions. Two players agree on a game and rules, place equal testnet deposits into non-custodial escrow, complete the match, and receive a verifiable payout or refund based on the recorded outcome. We are preparing a controlled Arc testnet pilot and are looking for gaming communities and tournament operators to help test onboarding, match operations, dispute handling, and settlement UX.

## What we are asking from a design partner

- Nominate 10–20 eligible adult testers.
- Coordinate a small set of structured 1v1 test matches.
- Use testnet assets only; no real-value deposit is required.
- Participate in a 30-minute kickoff and a 30-minute feedback review.
- Share structured feedback on onboarding, match rules, settlement, refunds, and trust.
- Permit anonymized aggregate pilot metrics to be included in grant reporting.

## What the partner receives

- Early access to the controlled Arc testnet pilot.
- Guided onboarding and direct support.
- Influence over community, tournament, and settlement workflows.
- A written pilot findings summary.
- Optional public acknowledgement as a design partner, only after written approval.

## Discovery call agenda

1. How the community currently organizes 1v1 challenges.
2. Where trust, payment, result, or dispute problems occur.
3. Typical participant profile, geography, games, and event frequency.
4. Pilot eligibility and safeguarding requirements.
5. Success metrics and operational responsibilities.
6. Whether the partner can provide a non-binding letter of intent.

## Evidence to retain for the grant

- Partner’s written permission to reference its name privately or publicly.
- Date and participants of the discovery call.
