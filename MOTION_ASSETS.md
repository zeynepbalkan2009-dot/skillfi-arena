# SkillFi Arena motion asset manifest

The Phase 1 waiting system uses four original, code-native SVG/CSS scenes authored for SkillFi Arena:

- Arena Spinner — abstract geometric spinning forms inside an arena ring.
- Five Stones Catch — generic stones and a geometric hand silhouette.
- Marble Knock — original circles, collision path, and impact pulse.
- Yo-Yo Pulse — concentric geometric disc, line, and rhythm pulse.

No third-party artwork, brands, characters, game footage, GIFs, videos, Lottie packages, or downloaded assets are included. Familiar physical-motion ideas are represented through new geometric artwork and do not reproduce a protected product design.

Scenes rotate every six seconds. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables both scene rotation and CSS animation. The assets are inline vectors, keeping the mobile payload small and avoiding extra network requests.
